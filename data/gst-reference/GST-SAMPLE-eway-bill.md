# SAMPLE — E-way bill teaching note

**SAMPLE STUDY NOTE for FirmBrain.AI Phase 1 RAG testing.**  
Thresholds, PIN-code exemptions, and transporter rules change. Confirm on the e-way bill portal and Rule 138 series.

## Document phrase: what is an e-way bill

An **e-way bill** is an electronic document required for movement of goods of consignment value **above the notified threshold**, generated on the GST e-way bill portal (or via registered devices), subject to exemptions.

## Document phrase: who generates it

Typically the **registered person who causes movement** generates the e-way bill. A **transporter** may generate it in specified cases if the supplier/recipient has not.

## Document phrase: Part A and Part B

Teaching split:

- **Part A:** invoice/challan details, GSTIN, consignment value, HSN, reason for transportation.
- **Part B:** transporter details / vehicle number, updated when the vehicle is assigned.

## Document phrase: validity

Validity is generally based on **distance** and the type of vehicle (over-dimensional cargo has different slabs). Expiry is computed from the time of generation as per rules.
