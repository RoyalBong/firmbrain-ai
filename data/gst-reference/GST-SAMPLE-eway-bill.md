# E-Way Bill

## What is an e-way bill

An electronic document required under Rule 138 of the CGST Rules for movement of goods with consignment value exceeding ₹50,000 (inter-state; some states set intra-state thresholds between ₹50,000–₹1 lakh). Generated on ewaybillgst.gov.in before movement begins.

## Who generates it

Typically the registered person who causes the movement (supplier or recipient). A transporter may generate it if the supplier/recipient hasn't, or complete Part B if the supplier only filled Part A.

## Part A and Part B

- **Part A**: consignment details — GSTIN of supplier/recipient, place of dispatch/delivery, invoice number and date, HSN code, value of goods, reason for transport.
- **Part B**: transport details — vehicle number or transporter ID.

**Important**: the e-way bill's validity clock starts only once Part B is filled — not at the time Part A/generation happens. For intra-state movement within 50 km (e.g., to a transporter's premises), Part B is not mandatory for that leg.

## Validity period (by distance)

| Distance | Regular cargo | Over-dimensional cargo |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 200 km | 1 day | 1 day |
| Each additional 200 km | +1 day | — |
| Over-dimensional cargo | — | 1 day per 20 km |

## Recent rule changes (effective January 2025 onward)
- **180-day document restriction**: e-way bills can only be generated for invoices/documents dated within 180 days of the generation date.
- **360-day extension cap**: total validity including all extensions cannot exceed 360 days from original generation.
- Extensions allowed within 8 hours before or after expiry, with a stated reason (breakdown, natural disaster, etc.).

## Penalty for non-compliance
Transporting goods without a valid e-way bill attracts a penalty under Section 129 — typically 200% of the tax payable (or ₹10,000, whichever is higher), plus possible detention of goods/vehicle.

## Source note
Based on Rule 138, CGST Rules, and CBIC e-way bill portal rules as of 2026, including the January 2025 rule changes. State-specific intra-state thresholds vary — always confirm on the e-way bill portal.
