## Document phrase: when registration is required

A supplier may be required to obtain GST registration when **aggregate turnover** in a financial year exceeds the notified threshold, or when a **compulsory registration** provision applies (for example certain e-commerce, reverse-charge, or interstate supply situations as currently drafted).

## Document phrase: aggregate turnover

**Aggregate turnover** is a defined GST term. Teaching definition: all taxable supplies, exempt supplies, exports, and inter-state supplies of persons with the same PAN, computed on an all-India basis, with inclusions/exclusions as per the Act (inward supplies under reverse charge are typically excluded from this particular definition — confirm in the Act).

## Document phrase: compulsory registration

Some persons must register **even if** turnover is below the threshold (illustrative teaching list — verify current Section 24):

- Persons making inter-state taxable supply (with notified exceptions, e.g. certain services).
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons.
- Persons required to pay tax under reverse charge.
- E-commerce operators / persons who supply through e-commerce operators (as specified).
- Persons who supply on behalf of other taxable persons (agents).
- Input service distributor.
- Persons who supply goods or services through an e-commerce operator who is required to collect tax at source, as specified.
