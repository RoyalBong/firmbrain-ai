# GST Registration Threshold

## When registration is required

A person must obtain GST registration when aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds the applicable threshold, or when a compulsory registration category under Section 24 applies (regardless of turnover).

## Current thresholds (by supply type and state category)

| Supplier type | Normal category states | Special category states |
|---|---|---|
| Goods only | ₹40 lakh | ₹20 lakh |
| Services, or mixed goods + services | ₹20 lakh | ₹10 lakh |

Special category states with lower thresholds include: Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Puducherry, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura, Uttarakhand (goods threshold ₹20 lakh in these states).

## Aggregate turnover (Section 2(6), CGST Act)

Aggregate turnover = all taxable supplies + exempt supplies + exports + inter-state supplies of persons with the same PAN, computed on an all-India basis. It excludes inward supplies taxed under reverse charge.

Aggregate turnover is calculated PAN-wide, across all states/GSTINs — not separately per state.

## Compulsory registration regardless of turnover (Section 24)

These categories must register from day one, irrespective of turnover:
- Persons making inter-state taxable supply (with limited exceptions for certain services)
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons
- Persons required to pay tax under reverse charge
- E-commerce operators, and persons supplying through an e-commerce operator required to collect TCS
- Input Service Distributors
- Agents supplying on behalf of other taxable persons

## Composition Scheme (related, not the same as registration threshold)

Businesses with aggregate turnover up to ₹1.5 crore (₹75 lakh in special category states) may opt for the Composition Scheme — simplified quarterly filing (CMP-08 + annual GSTR-4), fixed tax rate (1-6% depending on business type), but no ITC can be claimed.

## Source note
Based on CGST Act Section 22 (registration threshold) and Section 24 (compulsory registration), and current CBIC notifications as of 2026. Thresholds are subject to periodic notification — always verify current limits on the GST portal before advising a client.
