## Document phrase: conditions to claim ITC

For a registered person, ITC on inward supplies is generally available only when **all** of the following are satisfied (high-level teaching list):

1. The person holds a **tax invoice or debit note** (or other prescribed document).
2. The person has **received the goods or services**.
3. Tax charged has been **actually paid to the Government** (by the supplier, as evidenced through the prescribed mechanism).
4. The recipient has **furnished the return**.
5. Additional conditions in Section 16 (including time limits and any restriction on claiming credit after a specified date in the following year) must also be met as currently in force.

## Document phrase: books vs portal

ITC in books must be reconcilable with credit appearing in the **auto-drafted ITC statement** (commonly discussed as GSTR-2B in practitioner workflow). This sample does not replace GSTR-2B instructions.
