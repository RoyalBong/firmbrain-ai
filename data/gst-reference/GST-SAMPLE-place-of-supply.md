# SAMPLE — Place of supply (goods vs services, teaching)

**SAMPLE STUDY NOTE for FirmBrain.AI Phase 1 RAG testing.**  
Place of supply (POS) decides CGST+SGST vs IGST. This note only names the **default teaching rules**. There are many special POS rules (installation, events, immovable property, intermediary, online services, etc.).

## Document phrase: goods — default

For supply of **goods** (where the supply involves movement), the default teaching rule is: place of supply is the **location where the movement of goods terminates for delivery to the recipient**.

Where supply does **not** involve movement, the default teaching rule is: place of supply is the **location of the goods at the time of delivery to the recipient**.

## Document phrase: services — default

For supply of **services**, the default teaching rule (Section 13/12 as applicable for domestic vs cross-border) for a supply to a registered person is often taught as: place of supply is the **location of the recipient**. For an unregistered recipient, special rules apply (including location of recipient if an address exists on record, else location of supplier — confirm current section).

## Document phrase: IGST vs CGST+SGST

- If POS is in a **different state** from the supplier’s location: typically **IGST**.
- If POS is in the **same state**: typically **CGST + SGST/UTGST**.
