# Place of Supply (POS) — Goods vs Services

Place of supply determines whether a transaction attracts CGST+SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state/cross-border). Governed by Sections 10-13 of the IGST Act.

## Goods (Section 10, IGST Act)

- **Where movement is involved**: place of supply is the location where the goods are delivered to the recipient (movement terminates).
- **Where no movement is involved** (e.g., ex-works pickup): place of supply is the location of the goods at the time of delivery.

Example: Goods shipped from Gujarat to Maharashtra → place of supply is Maharashtra → IGST applies.

## Services — domestic (Section 12, IGST Act, both parties in India)

- **Registered recipient**: place of supply is the location of the recipient.
- **Unregistered recipient**: location of recipient if address is on record; otherwise, location of the supplier.

Special category overrides:
- Immovable property services: location of the property.
- Restaurant/catering/personal grooming/health services: location where performed.
- Event admission/organization: event location (or recipient location, if specified).

## Services — cross-border (Section 13, IGST Act, supplier or recipient outside India)

Default rule: location of the recipient. If recipient's location isn't available, location of the supplier.

Example: A Bengaluru software company provides IT consulting to a US client → place of supply is the US → zero-rated export of services.

## CGST+SGST vs IGST

- Place of supply in a **different state** from supplier's location → IGST
- Place of supply in the **same state** → CGST + SGST/UTGST

## Source note
Based on IGST Act Sections 10-13. Numerous special-case overrides exist (banking, telecom, transportation, insurance, etc.) beyond this summary — consult the Act directly for anything outside general goods/services rules.
