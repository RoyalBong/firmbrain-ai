# SAMPLE — GST return types (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9)

**SAMPLE STUDY NOTE for FirmBrain.AI Phase 1 RAG testing.**  
Not official CBIC/GSTN text. Not legal advice. Confirm against the GST portal and current notifications before any filing.

## Purpose

This note lists the **names** of common GST returns so a retrieval test can cite a known heading.

## GSTR-1

GSTR-1 is the **statement of outward supplies**. It reports invoices, credit/debit notes, and related outward supply details for a tax period.

## GSTR-3B

GSTR-3B is a **summary return** used to declare supplies, input tax credit (ITC) claimed, and tax payable, and to pay tax for the period (as applicable under current rules).

## GSTR-9

GSTR-9 is the **annual return** for registered persons (subject to eligibility and any exemption notified for a given financial year).

## GSTR-9C

GSTR-9C is the **reconciliation statement** (self-certified or as required by current law) that reconciles audited financials with GST annual return figures, where applicable.
