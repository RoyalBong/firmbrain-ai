# SAMPLE — Reverse charge (RCM) teaching note

**SAMPLE STUDY NOTE for FirmBrain.AI Phase 1 RAG testing.**  
RCM lists are notification-driven and change. Do not treat any example below as a current complete list.

## Document phrase: what is reverse charge

Under **reverse charge**, the **recipient** is liable to pay GST instead of the supplier, for notified categories of goods or services, or for supplies from an unregistered person where such liability is triggered by current law.

## Document phrase: self-invoice

When RCM applies and the supplier is not eligible to issue a tax invoice in the normal way, the recipient may need to issue a **self-invoice** and pay tax, then claim ITC subject to ordinary ITC conditions (if eligible).

## Document phrase: time of supply under RCM

Time of supply for RCM has **special rules** (often taught as earliest of payment date, or a prescribed number of days from invoice/debit note). Always check Section 12/13 RCM clauses in force.
