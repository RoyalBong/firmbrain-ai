# Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) Under GST

## What is reverse charge

Under reverse charge, the recipient (buyer) — not the supplier — is liable to pay GST directly to the government. The supplier does not charge, collect, or deposit GST on the invoice.

RCM applies under two triggers:
- **Section 9(3):** notified categories of goods/services (mandatory RCM regardless of supplier's registration status)
- **Section 9(4):** specified purchases from unregistered suppliers

## Common Section 9(3) services under RCM

| Service | Supplier | Recipient (pays RCM) | Typical GST rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goods Transport Agency (GTA), road transport | GTA not on forward charge | Factory, registered person, body corporate, partnership firm, society | 5% (no ITC to GTA) or 12% (GTA can claim ITC) |
| Legal services | Individual advocate / firm of advocates | Any business entity | 18% |
| Arbitral tribunal services | Arbitral tribunal | Any business entity | 18% |
| Sponsorship services | Any person (other than a body corporate) | Body corporate or partnership firm | 18% |
| Director's services (personal capacity, not as employee) | Director | The company/body corporate | 18% |

## Self-invoicing

When RCM applies and the supplier cannot issue a normal tax invoice (e.g., unregistered supplier), the recipient must issue a self-invoice and pay the tax themselves.

## ITC on RCM-paid tax

The recipient can claim ITC on RCM-paid GST (subject to ordinary ITC conditions) in the same return period in which the tax was paid — but the RCM liability itself must always be settled in cash via the Electronic Cash Ledger, never offset against existing ITC balance.

## Composition dealers and RCM

Composition scheme dealers must still pay GST under RCM on notified supplies and on all inward supplies from unregistered persons (Section 10(4)) — but cannot claim ITC on these RCM payments, since composition dealers can't claim ITC at all.

## Source note
Based on CGST Act Sections 9(3) and 9(4). RCM-applicable categories are notification-driven and change periodically — always verify the current notified list before applying RCM to a transaction.
