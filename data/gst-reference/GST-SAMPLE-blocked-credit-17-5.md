# SAMPLE — Blocked credit / ineligible ITC (Section 17(5) overview)

**SAMPLE STUDY NOTE for FirmBrain.AI Phase 1 RAG testing.**  
This is a **teaching summary** of commonly discussed blocked-credit categories. It is not the full text of CGST Act Section 17(5). Always read the Act, Rules, and circulars. “Block ITR” in firm jargon here means **blocked input tax credit**, not income-tax ITR.

## Document phrase: blocked credit

**Blocked credit** means ITC that the law **does not allow** even if the purchase appears to be for business, except where a specific exception in Section 17(5) applies.

## Common teaching categories (illustrative, not exhaustive)

Practitioners often review these buckets when screening ITC:

- Motor vehicles for transportation of persons (with statutory exceptions, e.g. further supply of such vehicles, or specified transport services).
- Food and beverages, outdoor catering, beauty treatment, health services, cosmetic and plastic surgery (with exceptions, e.g. inward supply used to make an outward supply of the same category, or as an element of a taxable composite/mixed supply).
- Membership of a club, health and fitness centre.
- Travel benefits to employees on vacation (such as leave or home travel concession).
- Works contract services for construction of an immovable property (except plant and machinery) except where the recipient is in the same line of business.
- Goods or services used for construction of an immovable property on own account (except plant and machinery).
- Goods or services on which tax is paid under composition (by the supplier, as applicable).
- Goods or services used for personal consumption.
- Goods lost, stolen, destroyed, written off, or disposed of by way of gift or free samples.
- Any tax paid under specified demand provisions (as listed in the Act).

## Exceptions exist

Every bullet above is **qualified by the Act**. Do not treat this list as a complete block. If a client fact pattern might fit an exception, read Section 17(5) and related circulars.
